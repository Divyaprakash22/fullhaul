The problem

The empty leg is the most expensive part of the trip.

Freight planning obsesses over the outbound delivery and forgets the truck still has to get home. That blind spot is where the money, fuel, and emissions disappear.

₹40,000 Cr+
Estimated annual empty-mile losses, India logistics
30–40%

1 in 3

How it works

One journey, tracked from the first mile.

Every step happens before the driver reaches the destination — not after the truck is already empty.

01Departure

A delivery is scheduled

The truck leaves with its outbound load. FullHaul already knows vehicle type, remaining capacity, destination, and expected arrival.

02In transit

AI predicts tomorrow's demand

Historical shipment patterns, seasonal demand and regional freight flow forecast where return cargo will originate.

03Before unload

Return loads are reserved

Before the truck reaches its destination, the system proposes matching return loads to the driver.

04Consolidation

Multiple shippers, one truck

Smaller loads from several nearby businesses are combined into one viable return trip — ideal for MSMEs.
